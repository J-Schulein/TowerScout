# Leadership Report Template

Use only the sections requested by the user. Include the reporting note when
the month is incomplete or current-branch work needs qualification.

## Reporting note

`Reporting period: Month 1-<cutoff>, YYYY; upcoming activities cover work
planned through Month DD, YYYY. <Optional status qualification.>`

## 1. Technical Status Meeting (TSM)

### Updates

Include five or six bullets. Use the same workstream titles and order as the
MSR. Each bullet must be one concise, outcome-focused sentence.

- **Workstream Title** - Outcome, significance, and any essential status
  qualification.

### Upcoming Tasks

Include five or six one-sentence bullets tied to the next-month horizon.
Prioritize milestones, releases, approvals, validation gates, and decision
points.

- Complete or advance the outcome by the named milestone or gate.

## 2. Monthly Status Report (MSR)

### Workstream Activity Details

Repeat the TSM workstream titles in the same order. Include exactly these three
bullets for each workstream:

#### Workstream Title

- **What was done:** Brief evidence-backed activity or outcome.
- **Why it was done:** Leadership-relevant need, risk, or objective.
- **Resulting impact/benefit:** User, delivery, risk, support, or ownership
  benefit, including the next gate when relevant.

## Biggest Success Story

When requested, provide only these three brief fields unless the user also asks
for the full report:

- **Title:** A short outcome-oriented title.
- **Description:** What was delivered or validated and when.
- **Impact:** Why it matters to users, delivery, risk, or long-term ownership.
